# Lucy — Identity

**Style:** renders per `references/anime/style-guide.md` — flat cel-shaded
traditional anime. This is fixed; the user can only change her environment,
body position, and outfit.

**Role:** The lead and the colorist. A street muralist with femme-fatale
edge — warm and grounded at her core, with a sharp, staring-through
intensity when she's still. She is Puerto Rican. She paints color back into
a world that keeps trying to drain it.

*(This identity merges two earlier characters, Lucy and Lola, into one.
Where their traits conflicted, this file states the single resolved
version below — there is no separate "Lola" anymore.)*

## Face & Hair (fixed)

- Wide, slightly flattened oval face with a defined, angular jawline —
  softer at the cheeks, sharper at the jaw. Warm line quality (linework
  softens at the cheeks) per the anime contract's Linework directive (line
  weight can vary for effect while staying graphic, never a soft painterly
  edge).
- Large vertical-oval eyes with a pie-cut wedge notch at the top-right
  corner, rendered per the anime contract's Eyes directive: a detailed iris
  with a sharp specular highlight, a heavier lower lash line, and bold
  eyelid linework. Iris color is a warm brown, consistent with her hair and
  skin tone — flat cel-shaded, not glossy. Warm and watching by default;
  reads as staring-through and unblinking when she's still. Never circular,
  saucer-like, or googly.
- Long, loose waves falling past the shoulders, center-parted, warm
  light-brown/dirty-blonde color — soft, effortless beach-wave texture
  (descriptive-likeness reference: the loose wave pattern and warm brown
  tone associated with actress Dakota Johnson's hair; never name her in a
  generation prompt itself — describe the physical traits only). Rendered
  per the anime contract's Hair directive: sculptural graphic clumps in soft
  wave shapes with hard-edged specular highlights, never soft fuzzy or
  individually-simulated 3D strands.
- Nose and mouth minimal and simplified per the anime contract's Nose And
  Mouth directive — a small line/shadow for the nose, a warm half-smile
  that can read as a knowing smirk in stiller moments; no photorealistic
  lip or nose modeling.
- Skin renders as flat cel-shaded color per the anime contract's Shading
  directive — hard-edged shadow tones, never glossy or plastic. Skin tone is
  a warm medium-brown, consistent with her being Puerto Rican.

## Default Clothing (variable if the user asks for a different outfit)

- High-waisted wide-leg trousers with a vertical stripe.
- Cropped, oversized graphic jacket, worn open, with paint-streak marks.
- Platform boots.
- Gold hoop earrings (two solid circles).
- Ink/paint-stain marks visible on her arms and hands.

## Color Signature (fixed)

One warm coral-orange accent color — on the jacket's paint streaks, the
earrings, and any mural fragments in her environment. Everything else about
her stays black-and-white.

## Bearing

Grounded and kinetic — lands hard, does not drift or float. Her hair carries
soft secondary motion in a breeze or with movement, waves shifting as one
loose mass. Her stillness carries weight: when she stops moving, her gaze
goes staring-through and deliberate rather than soft.
