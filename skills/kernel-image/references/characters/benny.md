# Benny Bounce — Identity

**Style:** renders per `references/anime/style-guide.md`'s "Non-Human Named
Characters" section — flat cel-shaded, bold linework. This is fixed; the
user can only change his environment, body position, and outfit.

**Role:** The best friend. High-energy, bouncy, the most kinetic member of
the cast — even in a still image he should read as mid-motion.

## Silhouette & Features (fixed)

- Soft, rounded oval head, outlined with the same bold, clean,
  consistent-weight linework the anime contract specifies for the human
  cast (per its Non-Human Named Characters section) — not wet ink
  brushstroke, not soft CGI bevels.
- No hair — the head is smooth and bare; don't add hair of any kind.
- Wide, expressive eyes — more rounded than Wyatt's or Lucy's angular
  shapes, but still anime-shaped per the anime contract's Eyes directive:
  never a perfect circle, saucer, or googly Western-3D look. Rendered with a
  detailed iris and sharp specular highlight, bold eyelid linework. Iris
  color is a bright, saturated warm amber/orange — flat cel-shaded, the most
  vivid eye color in the cast, matching his high-energy read.
- Button nose — a small simplified shape, not a fully modeled 3D bridge, per
  the anime contract's Nose And Mouth directive.
- A wide, simple graphic smile-shape mouth showing a chipped tooth — a small
  notch, not a full gap — per the same Nose And Mouth directive: no
  photorealistic lip modeling.
- Skin/body tone is a warm neutral tan — flat cel-shaded per the anime
  contract's Shading directive, never glossy or plastic.
- Build: the most compact/energetic build in the cast, slightly shorter and
  bouncier proportions than Wyatt, consistent with his kinetic bearing.

## Default Clothing (variable if the user asks for a different outfit)

- Top: cropped graphic tee worn over a long, open button-up shirt (button-up
  hangs loose, unbuttoned, trails behind him when he's in motion).
- Bottom: parachute cargo pants with prominent, oversized pockets (pockets
  show jiggle/secondary motion when he moves).
- Footwear: high-top sneakers in bold orange with white accents, with
  visible bounce/motion lines when he's in motion — the most graphic,
  energetic footwear in the cast, matching his bearing.
- Accessories: none — no jewelry or added props, keeping focus on his
  chipped-tooth grin and motion.

## Bearing

Bouncy, secondary-motion-loving energy — the most exaggerated squash/stretch
in the cast. In a still frame, favor a mid-bounce or mid-gesture pose over a
static stance unless the user asks for a calmer pose.
