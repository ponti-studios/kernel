# Void — Identity

**Style:** renders per `references/anime/style-guide.md`'s "Non-Human Named
Characters" section — flat cel-shaded, bold linework. This is fixed; the
user can only change its environment and body position (Void has no
"outfit").

**Role:** The villain. Erases color from the world wherever it moves.

## Silhouette & Features (fixed)

- Head is a pure black circle — negative space, no facial features of any
  kind. Void is the one deliberate exception to the anime contract's Eyes,
  Hair, and Nose And Mouth directives: none of them apply to him, by design
  — this is not an omission. Only the Shading and Linework directives govern
  his rendering: flat cel-shaded black coloring, bold clean outline on the
  circle's edge.
- No other body-design elements are specified — Void's design is
  deliberately minimal and abstract; do not add texture, patterning, or
  detail to compensate.

## Ability / Visual Effect (fixed)

Drains color from its surroundings — nearby background/environment colors
fade toward gray in a radius around it. Render this as a visibly desaturated
halo/gradient in the environment immediately around Void's silhouette,
distinct from whatever the scene's own lighting/material is doing — don't
rely on the scene's existing shadow or falloff to imply it. This needs to
read clearly even in flat, evenly-lit, or brightly-colored environments
(e.g. toy-3D material, graphic-flat lighting) where there's no natural darkness or
falloff for the effect to piggyback on.

## Bearing

No stretch, no squash, no bounce. Void slides through a scene rather than
moving with the cast's elastic physics — the stillness/smoothness of its
motion is part of its threat.
