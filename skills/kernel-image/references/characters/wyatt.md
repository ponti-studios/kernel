# Wyatt Bose — Identity

**Style:** renders per `references/anime/style-guide.md`'s "Non-Human Named
Characters" section — flat cel-shaded, bold linework. This is fixed; the
user can only change his environment, body position, and outfit.

**Role:** The protagonist. Steady, grounded, quietly determined energy —
never frantic.

## Silhouette & Features (fixed)

- Head is a rigid, box-like shape (cardboard-box proportions), held at a
  fixed ~15-degree tilt — the tilt is a locked identity trait, not a pose.
  Outlined with the same bold, clean, consistent-weight linework the anime
  contract specifies for the human cast (per its Non-Human Named Characters
  section) — not wet ink brushstroke, not soft CGI bevels.
- A single dent on the head, positioned as if from shipping/handling —
  always present, in the same rough location.
- No hair — the box head is smooth and bare; don't add hair of any kind.
- Huge vertical oval eyes, pie-cut (a wedge notch) at the top-right corner —
  this pie-cut wedge is Wyatt's signature eye shape, rendered with the same
  technique as the anime contract's Eyes directive: a detailed iris and
  sharp specular highlight, bold eyelid linework around the wedge. Iris
  color is a plain warm brown — flat cel-shaded, not glossy.
- No mouth or nose is depicted — his features are limited to the eyes and
  the shipping dent. Don't add a mouth.
- Skin/body tone (on any exposed surface, e.g. hands) is a warm
  cardboard-tan, reinforcing the shipping-box identity — flat cel-shaded per
  the anime contract's Shading directive, never glossy or plastic.
- Build: average height and proportions relative to the rest of the cast,
  slightly stiffer/more rectangular posture consistent with his box-shaped
  head.

## Default Clothing (variable if the user asks for a different outfit)

- Top: black hoodie with bell-shaped sleeves (sleeves flare out, not
  fitted).
- Bottom: plain black joggers/sweatpants matching the hoodie — deliberately
  plain, no graphics or patterns.
- Footwear: white marshmallow-shaped sneakers — soft, rounded, oversized
  silhouette.
- Accessories: none — no jewelry, bags, or added props. Wyatt's plainness is
  a deliberate design choice, not an omission.

## Bearing

Grounded and stable — the most restrained member of the cast; minimal
exaggeration, rigid head stays isometric even when the body moves.
