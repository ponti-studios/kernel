# Environment: Toy-Like 3D Material

Premium toy-like 3D material/geometry rendering — Pixar-level polish blended
with Apple product rendering: simple, friendly, controlled. This is a
**material** environment (how objects/creatures are built and surfaced), as
opposed to a lighting environment (`tenebrism.md`, or `lighting.md`'s Soft
Studio and Graphic Flat sections) — it's usually paired with `lighting.md`'s
Soft Studio section unless the user asks to blend a different one in (e.g.
dramatic tenebrist lighting on a toy-3D object).

Applies to non-human subjects only: creatures, mascots, objects, packaging,
sneakers. Per the skill's Universal Human Rendering Rule, any human figure
does NOT follow this file — it renders per `references/anime/style-guide.md`
instead. Named characters (`references/characters/`) never follow this file
either — their rendering is fixed regardless of which environment is active
around them.

## Style Overview

Create a high-quality 3D render in a minimalist, stylized aesthetic:
clean geometry, soft materials. Simple, friendly, and controlled.

## Object/Creature Design

- Slightly exaggerated proportions with a larger head and smaller body, if
  the subject has a head/body distinction.
- Rounded, simplified forms.
- No sharp edges; use smooth bevels.
- Simplified mitten-like hands, if the subject has hands.
- Minimal facial features, if the subject has a face:
  - small oval or dot eyes, solid color
  - subtle curved mouth
  - no eyelashes, detailed nose, or realism

## Materials

- Soft matte to satin finishes.
- Skin/surface (non-human creatures/mascots only): smooth, clay-like
  material with subtle shading.
- Clothing: soft, slightly rubberized cotton feel.
- Shoes: structured but soft, combining matte leather and foam-like
  materials.
- Avoid glossy or reflective surfaces.
- Avoid noisy or complex texture maps.

## Color

- Restrained, curated palette.
- Slightly desaturated but still rich.
- Avoid overly vibrant or clashing colors.

## Clothing

- Simplified real-world clothing.
- Minimal seams and stitching.
- Broad intentional folds, not realistic fabric simulation.

## Example: Panda-Style Sneakers

- Black and white low-top sneaker design inspired by Nike Dunk Low "Panda."
- Clean color blocking: white base with black overlays.
- Simplified details.
- Chunky laces with smooth cylindrical form.
- Minimal stylized perforated toe box.
- Thick sole with soft edges.
- Branding minimal or generic; avoid explicit logos.

## Rendering

- High resolution.
- Clean edges with strong anti-aliasing.
- No grain or noise.
- Pure or light neutral background unless scene requires otherwise.

## Composition

- Full-body or product-style framing.
- Slight perspective angle.
- Centered or balanced composition.

## Do Not

- Add unnecessary detail.
- Introduce realism in textures or anatomy.
- Use sharp edges — always smooth bevels.
- Apply glossy or reflective surfaces.
- Break cohesion across elements.
