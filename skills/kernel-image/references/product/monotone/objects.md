# MØNØTØNÉ — Objects Category

Home goods and desk objects: trays, plaques, bookends, candle holders,
small vessels — anything that isn't a book, a tech accessory, or a
leather good. Governed by `references/product/monotone/design-system.md`
— do not restate brand rules here, only object-specific staging and
composition detail. This is also the default category for a product idea
that doesn't cleanly fit `books.md`, `tech.md`, or `leather.md`.

## Master Prompt Template

Adapt this to the specific object and its neighborhood (palette, material,
and motif per design-system.md's Palette System and Motif Language):

---

Professional e-commerce product photography for a premium design-object
brand website.

The image showcases a single [OBJECT TYPE] in [NEIGHBORHOOD]'s signature
[COLOR] [MATERIAL], resting on a minimalist, light gray textured concrete
plinth against a clean, solid off-white studio background. [CAMERA ANGLE
— choose the angle that best shows the object's defining silhouette and
any debossed/engraved detail].

**Design Details:** The object's surface carries a fine-line, minimalist
[MOTIF] subtly debossed or etched into the material. Any wordmark or
neighborhood name is debossed or engraved directly into the material,
small and discreet, never printed or foil-stamped. A small, premium
brushed metal brand plaque engraved with "MØNØTØNÉ" sits beside the
object on the plinth.

**Detail Grid (lower third of image):** A clean grid of two to three
close-up detail shots calling out the object's specific craft
details — material grain, debossing depth, edge/corner finish, or joinery.

**Lighting & Style:** Soft, diffused studio lighting with gentle,
realistic shadows that accentuate the object's material texture and the
depth of any debossing. High-end, sophisticated aesthetic, perfectly
sharp focus, ultra-clean web-ready assets, 8K resolution.

## Variation Patterns

| Variation | Adjustment |
|---|---|
| Paired/set | Show a coordinated pair or small set (e.g. bookend pair) rather than a single object, staggered slightly like the books stack |
| Detail-only | Drop the full-object hero shot, expand the detail grid to fill the frame |
| In-use lifestyle | Replace the concrete plinth with the object in a styled shelf, desk, or console scene reflecting the brand world |
| Cross-category pairing | Stage the object alongside a same-neighborhood product from another category (e.g. a SILVER LAKE tray beside the SILVER LAKE book) to reinforce the shared neighborhood identity |
