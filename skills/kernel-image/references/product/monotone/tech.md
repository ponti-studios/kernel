# MØNØTØNÉ — Tech Accessories Category

Cases, stands, chargers, cable organizers, desk-tech accessories.
Governed by `references/product/monotone/design-system.md` — do not
restate brand rules here, only tech-specific staging and composition
detail. This category has the most overlap with the Braun reference in
design-system.md's Brand Thesis — lean into functional minimalism over
ornament more than any other category.

## Master Prompt Template

Adapt this to the specific product and its neighborhood (palette,
material, and motif per design-system.md's Palette System and Motif
Language). Tech-accessory materials skew toward brushed metal and matte
soft-touch surfaces rather than cloth — pick whichever on-brand material
(per design-system.md's Material Honesty Rules) suits the specific
product's function:

---

Professional e-commerce product photography for a premium design-tech
brand website.

The image showcases a single [PRODUCT TYPE] in [NEIGHBORHOOD]'s signature
[COLOR] [MATERIAL — e.g. brushed aluminum, matte soft-touch composite],
resting on a minimalist, light gray textured concrete plinth against a
clean, solid off-white studio background. [CAMERA ANGLE — choose the
angle that best shows the product's functional form and any port/edge
detail]. The form is reduced to only what the object's function requires
— no visible ornament, no unnecessary seams or parting lines.

**Design Details:** A fine-line, minimalist [MOTIF] is subtly etched or
engraved into an inconspicuous surface of the product — never
interrupting a functional surface. Any wordmark or neighborhood name is
engraved directly into the material, small and discreet. A small, premium
brushed metal brand plaque engraved with "MØNØTØNÉ" sits beside the
product on the plinth.

**Detail Grid (lower third of image):** A clean grid of two to three
close-up detail shots calling out precision details — seam tolerances,
port/connector finish, button or dial machining, edge chamfer.

**Lighting & Style:** Soft, diffused studio lighting with gentle,
realistic shadows and clean specular highlights that read as precision
manufacturing rather than mass-market plastic. High-end, sophisticated
aesthetic, perfectly sharp focus, ultra-clean web-ready assets, 8K
resolution.

## Variation Patterns

| Variation | Adjustment |
|---|---|
| Paired set | Show a coordinated pair (e.g. charger + cable) staged together rather than a single item |
| Desk flatlay | Overhead flatlay on a desk surface (raw oak or concrete) with the product mid-use alongside a MØNØTØNÉ book or object from the same neighborhood |
| In-hand lifestyle | Product held or in active use by a human model — model renders per the Universal Human Rendering Rule (design-system.md's Photography Direction), product stays photoreal |
| Exploded/functional detail | Detail grid shows the product's functional state (e.g. cable coiled, stand extended) rather than static material close-ups |
