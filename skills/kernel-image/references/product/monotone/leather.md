# MØNØTØNÉ — Leather Goods Category

Bags, wallets, cardholders, and other small leather goods. Governed by
`references/product/monotone/design-system.md` — do not restate brand
rules here, only leather-specific staging and composition detail. This
category has the most overlap with the Hermès reference in
design-system.md's Brand Thesis — hand-craft signals (visible stitching,
edge-paint/burnishing, natural grain) matter more here than in any other
category.

## Master Prompt Template

Adapt this to the specific product and its neighborhood (palette,
material, and motif per design-system.md's Palette System and Motif
Language). Leather is always full-grain, with visible natural grain and
a hand-finished edge — never corrected-grain, never a printed or
embossed faux-grain texture (per design-system.md's Material Honesty
Rules):

---

Professional e-commerce product photography for a premium leather-goods
brand website.

The image showcases a single [PRODUCT TYPE] in [NEIGHBORHOOD]'s signature
[COLOR] full-grain leather, resting on a minimalist, light gray textured
concrete plinth against a clean, solid off-white studio background.
[CAMERA ANGLE — choose the angle that best shows the product's silhouette,
hardware, and stitching]. Visible saddle-stitching along the seams,
hand-burnished edges, and natural grain variation in the leather signal
hand-craftsmanship.

**Design Details:** A fine-line, minimalist [MOTIF] is subtly debossed
into an interior panel or gusset — visible but not the focal point of the
exterior. Any wordmark or neighborhood name is debossed directly into the
leather, small and discreet, near a seam or the interior lining, never a
printed exterior logo. Hardware (buckles, zippers, clasps) is brushed
metal, unbranded or minimally marked. A small, premium brushed metal
brand plaque engraved with "MØNØTØNÉ" sits beside the product on the
plinth.

**Detail Grid (lower third of image):** A clean grid of two to three
close-up detail shots calling out craft details — stitch density, edge
burnishing, hardware finish, or the debossed motif itself.

**Lighting & Style:** Soft, diffused studio lighting with gentle,
realistic shadows that accentuate the leather's natural grain and the
depth of the stitching and debossing. High-end, sophisticated aesthetic,
perfectly sharp focus, ultra-clean web-ready assets, 8K resolution.

## Variation Patterns

| Variation | Adjustment |
|---|---|
| Open/interior shot | Show the product open, revealing the interior lining, debossed motif panel, and any interior pockets/organization |
| Paired set | Show a coordinated pair (e.g. wallet + cardholder) staged together |
| In-hand / worn lifestyle | Product carried or worn by a human model — model renders per the Universal Human Rendering Rule (design-system.md's Photography Direction), product stays photoreal |
| Aging/patina detail | Detail grid includes a close-up suggesting how the leather will patina over time — a craft claim specific to full-grain leather |
