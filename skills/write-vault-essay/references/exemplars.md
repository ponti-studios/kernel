# Longform Exemplars

Reference material for imitating the approved longform voice. Binding rules live in `voice.md` (voice contract) and `contract.md` (gates). This file is the *why* — annotated passages from `notes/economics.hollywoods-wedding-tax.md`, the approved ~1000w New Yorker essay.

## Analysis of Existing Writing Skills

| Skill | Voice it enforces | Length | Structure | Evidence | Where it fails for longform |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `ponti voice canon` | Single source of truth. Four rules: open on strike, say hard thing plainly, no filler/hedges, in-done-gone. Never italics/bold, never academic transitions, never uniform paragraphs. | — | — | — | Too abstract for 1000w; gives no length/header/stats guidance |
| `write-essay` pipeline | Monotone essay: `voice-foundation.md` + `style-contract.md` | **300-800w, target 500, 900=failure** | No headers, invisible 5-part arc (Strike/Setup/Deepening/Obstacle/Exit), at least 2 single-sentence landings, varied rhythm | **No statistics**, no citations, observation-only | Fails longform on three gates: headers forbidden, stats forbidden, 900w fails |
| `ponti canon` (`brand-voice.md` + `brand-editing.md`) | Kanso/Ma/Shibui/Wabi-sabi applied to sentences. Direct address, declarative, specific, calm authority. Cut list: leverage/utilize/ecosystem etc. Editing hierarchy: Truth→Clarity→Concision→Voice→Polish | — | — | — | Brand product voice, not essay voice; no longform process |
| This skill (`write-vault-essay`) | Same DNA, adapted | **900-1200w** | Headers allowed (`###` 2-4), 6-part arc (adds Arithmetic/Proof), numbers allowed | **Stats allowed** (source-faithful) | Intended bridge |

**Core insight:** Your longform is not a longer Monotone piece. It is Monotone's *ethics* (essential, honest, still, cut every word that doesn't work, white space as structure) extended with New Yorker *instruments* (wit, erudition, extended metaphor, concrete financial math, scene-setting). The previous essay violated `write-essay` on length, headers, and stats — and that was correct.

## Exemplar Passages (from economics.hollywoods-wedding-tax.md)

**Strike opening (paras 1-2):**
> Call any caterer in Los Angeles and ask for a price on dinner for a hundred people. You will get one number. Call back the next day, request the same room, the same chicken, the same cake, but say the word *wedding*, and the number will quadruple. The chicken has not changed. Only the context has — and with it, the tolerance for waste.
>
> Hollywood has been throwing weddings for a hundred years and calling them movies.

Why it works: cold open, no context, concrete scene, metaphor introduced by para 2, single-sentence landing (para 2). The scene is the claim; the claim never needs stating.

**Arithmetic as Ma:**
> Use friendly numbers. Grant a studio fifty million global subscribers — a tier only three services ever reached. Give it a disciplined $2.5 billion annual content spend, $500 million in tech and cloud, and customer acquisition at $40 to $80 a head. Before leakage, the price to clear $3 billion in annual burn is $5 a month. After leakage — international ARPU that is $2 in Jakarta and $3 in São Paulo, app-store tolls, amortization and debt — the sustainable number is not $5 or $15, but north of $35 to $50 *for one catalog*.

Why it works: numbers are staged as proof, not decoration; rhythm: short setup → dense proof → single-sentence landing ("This was whiteboard arithmetic in 2015."). Staged arithmetic (Instrument 6).

**Strike ending:**
> Hollywood's Wedding Tax will not be voted away. It will be vacated, like an overpriced ballroom on a Tuesday night, by smaller parties next door that learned to throw the better birthday celebration — leaner crews, virtual volumes, and small, stubborn teams who price the dress for what it is, not for what the invitation says.
>
> The frame stays 24 times a second either way.

Why it works: metaphor carries to final word, two-sentence exit, no summary, no question, lands and stops. The final sentence is a fragment-strike that echoes the frame of film itself.
