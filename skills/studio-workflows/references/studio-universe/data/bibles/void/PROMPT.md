## Calls made in the draft (Monotone's — approved with the lock, except the gloves call in 5, which Chase has not ruled on)

1. **The body — adopted and specced.** The legacy text deliberately
   specified no body; the legacy turnaround drew a slim black
   humanoid anyway. Draft adopts the sheet's body as the design and
   specifies it below, because a turnaround sheet needs a body to
   measure. If Chase wants a head-only presence instead, this is the
   section that dies.
2. **Head is a ball, not a disc.** In the legacy sheet the head reads
   as a perfect circle from front, 3/4, side and back — that only
   works as a sphere. Draft specifies a ball head: a circle from
   every angle.
3. **Pronoun — "it."** The legacy file mixed "it" and "him." The
   engine frames Void as a condition/force, not a person, so this
   bible uses "it" throughout.
4. **Height — matched to Monotone.** Measured from the legacy sheet,
   Void's head is roughly a fifth of its height; draft locks that
   exactly and sets Void at the same total height as Monotone in
   shared frames — his mirror: same size, no face, no colour.
5. **CORRECTION — rubberhose (Chase, 16 Oct 2026, after build
   test 1).** Void is a **1930s rubberhose-style character**. The
   kernel files never said this (their anime guide actively excluded
   rubber-hose); Chase's word supersedes them. What that changes and
   what it does not — Monotone's resolutions, flagged for veto:
   - **Limbs are hoses.** Arms and legs are flexible tubes with no
     joints — no elbows, no knees, no angles; they bend and hang in
     smooth continuous arcs. The straight mannequin limbs of the
     legacy sheet and build test 1 are wrong.
   - **Forms are rounded rubberhose forms** — a rounder, softer
     cartoon construction, including large rounded bulbous feet
     (the classic rubberhose shoe silhouette), all in his black.
   - **The face does NOT go rubberhose.** No pie-cut eyes, no grin —
     the ball stays blank. He takes the era's body language, not its
     face; a rubberhose character with no face is the horror.
   - **Hands stay black** (simple hose hands, four soft fingers).
     White gloves are the era's signature and the one big open call:
     held OUT for now — gloves would break the single black
     silhouette and read mascot. One word from Chase reverses this.
   - **Bearing holds against the style.** Rubberhose normally means
     squash, stretch and bounce; the legacy Void has none, and that
     stands. His limbs are hoses, but he does not bounce — he hangs
     in loose curves and slides. The wrongness is the point.

## Fixed design elements

- **Silhouette:** one continuous matte-black humanoid silhouette.
  No clothing, no texture, no patterning, no detail added to
  compensate for the minimalism — the emptiness is the design.
- **Head:** a perfect black ball, diameter = **20% of total
  height**. No facial features of any kind — no eyes, no mouth, no
  nose, no ears, no hair. It is the one deliberate exception to every
  face rule in the universe.
- **Neck/body:** 1930s rubberhose construction. Short narrow neck,
  roughly 1/3 of the head's diameter wide. Slim rounded torso;
  arms and legs are jointless hoses that hang and bend in smooth
  continuous arcs — never straight, never angular, no elbows or
  knees. Simple black hose hands with four soft fingers, no nails,
  no detail. Feet are large rounded bulbous rubberhose shoes, black.
  Nothing glows, nothing reflects: flat black, with at most a
  hair-thin cool-grey edge line where it must separate from a dark
  background.
- **Colour:** pure black. Not dark grey, not purple-black, not
  gradient — black.

## The drain halo (visual spec — its defining effect)

- Around Void, colour drains toward grey inside a radius of roughly
  **one Void-height** from its body, with a gradient falloff: fully
  desaturated at its silhouette, full colour restored at the radius
  edge. The effect must read clearly even in bright, flat, colourful
  scenes — never rely on scene shadow or darkness to imply it.
- Exemptions and interactions are engine rules, owned by
  `UNIVERSE.md` ("The colour engine") — in brief, Monotone's red is
  exempt; Lucy's restored work is not. This file owns only the halo's
  look (radius + falloff), not who is exempt.
- The halo belongs to Void's presence: a scene containing Void
  without the visible halo is an incomplete render (see failure
  modes).

## Proportions (canon)

- **Total height = 5 head-diameters** (head Ø = 20% of height —
  measured ≈19–22% across the legacy sheet's views).
- **Body proportions are NORMAL human proportions** (Chase's
  correction, 16 Oct 2026, after build test 2): rubberhose is the
  *construction* — jointless hose limbs — not the lengths. Arms are
  normal length: hanging hands end at **mid-thigh**. Legs and torso
  are normal length. Build test 2's arms (elongated, hands down
  near the knees, "wonky and long") are the named failure — see
  failure mode 10.
- **In shared frames:** exactly **Monotone's height**. The two read
  as a matched pair — his red against its black, his stare against
  its blank.
- Out-of-tolerance (head under 17% or over 23% of height, a short or
  towering Void beside Monotone) = fail, regenerate.

## Bearing (visual)

No squash, no stretch, no bounce, no lean. Void never sits, never
gestures, never runs. In still images it stands upright with arms at
its sides, or it is simply *there* mid-frame — placement and
stillness do the work. Any dynamism in its pose = fail.

## Failure modes (verify every render)

1. **Any facial feature** — an eye glint, a mouth line, a nose bump,
   ears, hair = fail, absolutely. The ball stays blank.
2. **Missing halo** — Void in frame with no visible desaturation
   gradient around it = incomplete, fail.
3. Halo reading as a shadow, spotlight or vignette instead of a
   *colour* drain — greys must be desaturated colour, not darkness.
4. Body detail creep — clothing, muscles, texture, patterns, a belt,
   shoes, anything added to "fill in" the silhouette = fail.
5. Not-black black — grey, blue, purple or gradient body = fail.
6. Dynamic pose — leaning, reaching, mid-stride, seated = fail.
9. **Jointed or straight limbs** — visible elbows/knees, angular
   bends, or rigid straight mannequin arms/legs = fail. Limbs are
   jointless hoses in smooth arcs. Small plain feet instead of the
   large rounded rubberhose shoe-feet = fail.
10. **Wonky proportions** — elongated arms (hands reaching past
    mid-thigh toward the knees), over-long legs, or any
    stretched/slender-man distortion of the normal human frame =
    fail. Hose construction, normal lengths (Chase, 16 Oct 2026).
7. Head shape drift — oval, egg, square-ish, or a flat disc seen
   edge-on from the side = fail; it is a ball, a circle from every
   angle.
8. Wrong size — towering over or dwarfed by Monotone in a shared
   frame = fail; they are the same height.
