## Fixed design elements (do not change)

- **Style:** 3D cartoon man, soft Pixar-like render. (He is an adult;
  the name "Masked Chibi" is kept, the toddler build is not.)
- **Proportions (LOCKED 17 Oct 2026; height AMENDED 18 Oct
  2026):** exactly **5 heads tall, 153cm** — his head, hair and
  mask included, is 20% of his total height (working tolerance
  18–22%). Adult construction throughout: legs ≈ half his
  height, adult torso, shoulders and hands. In shared frames he
  stands at exactly Akane's height — eye line to eye line —
  and 90% of Lucy's (cast heights chart, `UNIVERSE.md`) — an
  adult man level with Akane, never waist-high, never a mascot
  at her knees. (The 17 Oct lock's 122cm / 80%-of-Akane staging
  is superseded; posted work keeps it historically.)
- **Hair:** Voluminous black curly hair, medium length, covers forehead and ears.
- **Mask:** Matte black FULL-FACE mask in the style of a hockey mask
  (Jason Voorhees silhouette) — it covers the ENTIRE face, forehead to chin,
  cheek to cheek. No skin is visible around the mask's edges. Two ROUND eye
  holes, symmetric, positioned like a domino mask's holes — roughly equal
  size, spaced apart, centered horizontally. The holes are CUT OUT of the
  mask, revealing only pitch-black emptiness behind them. The eye holes are
  PITCH-BLACK EMPTY VOIDS — hollow darkness, nothing inside. NO eyes of any
  kind are ever visible: no human eyes, no eyelids, no eye whites, no pupils,
  no eye-like shapes. The holes are never painted-on eyes on the mask's
  surface, and the mask is never a solid blindfold-style band with no holes.
  The mask is opaque; you cannot see through it. Small dark strap buckles at
  both sides and one at the top center. The mask has ONLY the two eye holes —
  no ventilation dots, no perforations, no other openings of any kind. A
  subtle nose contour is part of the style. No mouth detail. Never change
  the mask shape or its full-face coverage.

### Mask failure modes (check for all of these in verification)

1. Human or cartoon eyes visible through the holes — the classic failure.
2. Eyes painted/printed ON the mask surface instead of holes cut INTO it.
3. Blindfold-style solid band with no holes at all.
4. Holes the wrong shape (almond, oval slits) instead of round.
5. Asymmetric holes — different sizes or heights.
6. Light or skin tone visible inside the holes — the holes must read as
   looking into a dark empty room, never as a lit interior.
7. Mask shrunk to a domino/eye-mask leaving forehead, cheeks, or chin
   exposed — it must cover the entire face.
8. Ventilation dots, perforations, or any extra openings beyond the two eye
   holes — the mask has only the eye holes.
9. Tattoo portraits facing outward or both facing the same way — the king
   and queen always face inward, toward each other.
10. Baby proportions — a head larger than ~22% of total height, toddler
    limbs, or any read younger than an adult man = fail. He is 5 heads
    tall (proportions lock, 17 Oct 2026); the retired ~3-head chibi
    build must never come back.
- **Neck tattoos:** TWO portrait tattoos, always this exact pairing and
  placement — a crowned KING (bearded man in profile) on the character's LEFT
  side of the neck, and a crowned QUEEN (woman in profile) on the character's
  RIGHT side of the neck. Both portraits ALWAYS face INWARD, toward the center
  of the throat — they face each other. Never two kings, never two queens,
  never facing outward, never both facing the same direction.
- **Outfit:** Black knit button cardigan (5 buttons, patch pockets) over a
  plain white crew t-shirt. Black cargo pants with side flap pockets.
  Black Adidas Samba sneakers: white side stripes, gum-brown soles,
  small blue heel tab.
- **Pose (canonical):** Mid-stride, dynamic, full body. Feet point forward,
  never splayed outward ("duck footed").
