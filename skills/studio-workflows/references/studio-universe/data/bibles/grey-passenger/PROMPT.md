## GREY PASSENGER BLOCK (stitch verbatim into every prompt the costume appears in)

"The Grey Passenger is a gaunt figure about 225cm tall: a floor-length greatcoat of layered ash-grey railway wool with a high collar, brass buttons dull with age, and a long ticket-punch chain at the belt; a smooth blank oval face of matte light grey, featureless except two deep hollow black oval eyes and a small dark open oval mouth — plainly a theatrical costume mask; a tall grey peaked cap with a cracked patent visor; very long draped sleeves that completely hide the hands; one hand always raised carrying a dim green-shaded inspector's lantern; a faint haze of coal dust around its hem. It is a person in a railway costume: a solid figure standing on its own feet, the greatcoat dense opaque wool with its hem resting on the ground, dressed for a harmless theatrical fright."
