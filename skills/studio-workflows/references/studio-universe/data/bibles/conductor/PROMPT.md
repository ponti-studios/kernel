# The Conductor — costume bible (Oxford Last Bus set, "The Last Bus")

> A costume, not a citizen. Void wears it in Frames 1, 3 and 4. In Frame 4
> the costume is off its wearer, the cap is held high by Wyatt, and Void
> stands revealed at his true height. This file is the single home of the
> costume prose. The core block goes into every frame the costume appears
> in; at most one named tail is appended per frame, picked by the frame

## CONDUCTOR BLOCK (stitch verbatim into every prompt the costume appears in)

<!-- PROMPT-BLOCK-START: conductor -->
"The Conductor is a tall, narrow figure about 196cm high: a black London Transport bus conductor's double-breasted coat with silver buttons, a high collar and long skirts to the ankle; a white shirt and black tie; black trousers with a thin red side stripe; raised black leather boots; white cotton gloves; and a tall black peaked cap with a polished patent visor and a blank oval badge. The face is a smooth porcelain oval mask the colour of a blank ticket, its only opening a single clean circular punch hole where the mouth would sit, and its surface blank ticket-white everywhere beyond that — plainly a theatrical costume mask. A heavy brass ticket punch hangs from a chain at the belt. It is a person in a bus conductor's costume: a solid figure standing on its own feet, the coat dense opaque wool, dressed for a harmless theatrical fright."
