## Failure modes (verify every render)

1. **Pie-cut / notch / crescent in her eyes** — that signature is
   Monotone's. Hers are plain green eyes with a heavy lower lash.
   Any wedge = fail.
2. Coral spreading beyond her accents — a coral outfit, coral hair,
   coral lips, or coral everything = fail. Coral is her design
   accent (a jacket streak, a pocket square) and her work's
   signature token, nothing else — never on her skin or face.
3. Gold hoops missing, doubled, or turned coral = fail.
4. Wardrobe drift — an outfit that is not the default or one of
   the rotation outfits in this bible, coloured clothing beyond
   the single coral accent, or any footwear other than plain
   black leather loafers (no platform boots, no sneakers) =
   fail. (The A-line skirt + sheer tights now belong to the
   Classic rotation outfit only — wardrobe set 17 Oct 2026.)
5. Marked skin or a made-up face — ink or pigment marks on her
   forearms or hands, or any makeup or coloured line on her face
   = fail; her skin is clean and her face unadorned (marks and
   makeup removed from her spec, 18 Oct 2026).
6. Hair straightened, shortened, lightened to brown or blonde, or
   rendered as individual strands = fail. (Her hair is BLACK —
   redesign, 17 Oct 2026.)
7. Skin darkened back to medium brown, or greyed outside a Void drain
   effect = fail. (Her skin is the light warm tan of the canonical
   build — redesign, 17 Oct 2026.)
8. Generic "model" beauty-face — she keeps the flattened oval face
   and angular jaw; a narrow pointed-chin face = fail.
