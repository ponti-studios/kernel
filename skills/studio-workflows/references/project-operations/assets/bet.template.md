## Problem

What is broken, for whom, and how do we know? Describe the pain without proposing a solution. Include any signals — customer interviews, support tickets, usage data, market trends — that suggest the problem is real.

## Why Now

What has recently changed — in technology, user behavior, or the market — that makes this the right moment to solve this? Without a "why now," the opportunity is timeless, which is a weakness.

## Who This Is For

Be specific. Not "busy professionals" — name the archetype, the context, the trigger moment. Who experiences this problem most acutely?

## Why Us

What makes this ours to solve? An existing user base, a technical capability, a distribution channel, a unique insight. If we don't have a credible answer here, that's worth flagging.

## Existing Alternatives

What do people do today? Why is that insufficient? This should be one tight paragraph — not a competitive matrix.

## Appetite

How much time and investment is this idea worth before we know if it's real?

- [ ] Spike / prototype (1–2 weeks)
- [ ] Small bet (2–6 weeks)
- [ ] Medium bet (1–3 months)
- [ ] Large bet (3+ months)

## Concept

Directional description of what this might be — no wireframes, no requirements. Think "fat marker sketch in words." What is the core interaction or experience that makes this valuable?

## Success Looks Like

Outcomes, not outputs. What would need to be true — in user behavior, business metrics, or qualitative signal — for this to be considered successful?

## Open Questions & Assumptions

What do we believe to be true that hasn't been validated? What would we need to learn before committing to a PRD?
