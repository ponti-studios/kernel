# North-star

## Project north Star

**tagline:** reclaiming the "mental minute" for the modern professional.

**target:** 500,000 active users | **price:** $30/year | **model:** direct-to-consumer (web-first)

### The Vision

we're building the personal operating system for the world's most influential consumers. we turn a morning of information noise into two minutes of executive signal.

### The Problem

**the burden of choice.** affluent urban consumers are drowning. 20+ premium newsletters, complex household logistics, high-stakes social networks.

**the pain:** "mental redline." the constant anxiety of missing a key insight or failing a social obligation.

**current solutions:** manual scrolling (doesn't work) or hiring personal assistants ($100k/year). both suck.

### The Solution

**the daily signal.** a proprietary ai layer that acts like your personal chief of staff.

- **hyper-personalized** — it synthesizes their news, their schedule, their network. not generic summaries.
- **frictionless** — delivered as a 2-minute daily briefing (audio or text) designed for the urban commute.

### Why Now?

- **llm cost collapse** — deep synthesis that cost $50 per user last year now costs $0.50.
- **app store shift** — regulatory changes let us bypass the 30% apple tax. margins move from 65% to 94%.
- **information fatigue** — peak content has hit a breaking point. curation is now more valuable than creation.

### Market Analysis

**target audience: affluent urban consumers**

the problem is information overload. managing household logistics and social capital is exhausting.

the solution is an ai-powered daily briefing that synthesizes your interests, the news, and your logistics into a 2-minute actionable signal.

willingness to pay is high. at $30/year, this is an impulse purchase for this demographic. it's "lifestyle infrastructure."

**market size:**

- **tam:** 50m+ global affluent urban knowledge workers.
- **target:** 500,000 users (1% of the us affluent segment).
- **revenue potential:** $15m arr at $30/year (impulse-buy price point).

### The "unfair" Business Model

web-first acquisition model with direct-to-consumer economics.

- **direct billing** — 97% margin via stripe (vs. 70% through app store).
- **high efficiency** — ai processing costs less than 2% of revenue.
- **scalable team** — designed to run at $1.5m arr per employee.

### Financial Model

shift from $200/hour consulting to a scalable, high-margin ai asset with a goal of $7.9m net profit annually with a lean team of 10.

stripe (3%) instead of the app store (30%) means you keep an extra $4.05m in annual profit.

### Unit Economics

llms scale nearly infinitely with negligible variable costs.

- **subscription price:** $30
- **acquisition cost (target cac):** $10 (paid ads + organic mix)
- **annual llm/data cost:** $0.50–$1.00 (summarization & rag)
- **ltv (lifetime value):** ~$90 (assuming 3-year retention)
- **ltv:cac ratio:** 9:1 (industry gold standard is 3:1)

### Organizational Structure

a 10-person team lets you step out of code and into the ceo role.

- **engineering (4):** 1 lead, 2 product devs, 1 infrastructure/ai specialist
- **growth (3):** 1 performance marketer (managing $1.25m spend), 1 content/seo, 1 lifecycle/email
- **customer success (2):** managing high-touch support for affluent users
- **ops/product (1):** daily sprint management and vendor relationships

### Strategic Milestones

**phase 1 (mvp):** build the web-to-app flow. secure direct billing infrastructure.

**phase 2 (growth):** aggressive cac testing. scale spend until $10 cac is verified.

**phase 3 (optimization):** introduce "household os" features to increase retention.

**phase 4 (exit):** prepare for acquisition at 5x–8x ebitda multiple ($50m+ exit).

### Risks & Mitigations

**app store friction** — use high-value web content (newsletters/blog) to capture credit cards before directing users to download the app.

**llm hallucinations** — implement "source citations" in the briefing so affluent users can verify facts instantly.

**churn** — focus on "daily habit" triggers (morning notifications) to make the app part of users' non-negotiable routine.

### The Ask

seeking investment to accelerate phase 2 (growth).

**milestone 1:** scale to 100k users.

**milestone 2:** finalize the "household os" feature set.

**milestone 3:** achieve $3m arr run rate within 12 months.

### The Team

led by a veteran consultant and technologist with experience in converting high-cost manual processes into low-cost automated assets. lean team of 10 elite ai and growth engineers.
