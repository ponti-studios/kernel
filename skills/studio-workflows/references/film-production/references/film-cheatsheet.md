# Film Craft Cheatsheet

_Quick reference for the set and the suite._

These checklets condense the full film craft standards into decision cards. For deeper treatment of any area, see the linked documents.

---

## Composing a Frame

```
□ Does every element serve the story?
□ Where does the eye land first? Is that where I want it?
□ Is the camera still? If not, why?
□ Is the color palette controlled? What is the accent?
□ Would the character notice what I am showing?
```

See [[film-capture]] for the full frame and camera standards.

---

## Approaching a Scene

```
□ Whose scene is this? (subjective ownership)
□ What does the character feel? (camera, color, sound should reflect this)
□ What would the character notice? (coverage decisions)
□ What is the minimum number of shots required?
```

See [[film-philosophy]] for directorial modes and [[film-production-design]] for world-building.

---

## The Edit

```
□ Did I hold the reaction long enough?
□ Is there a beat of silence before the next sound?
□ Does the music serve the character or the scene?
□ Does this cut exist because the story needs it, or because I am used to cutting here?
□ Am I dissolving out of uncertainty or out of narrative intent?
```

See [[film-editing]] and [[film-sound]] for the full post-production standards.
