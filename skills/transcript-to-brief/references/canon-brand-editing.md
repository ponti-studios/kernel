# Brand Editing

_The quality gate._

This document is the standard against which we measure every piece of copy before it ships. It exists to be placed in front of anyone reviewing output — including yourself.

For the philosophy and voice that inform these rules, see [[brand-voice]]. For copy mechanics and context-specific guidance, see [[brand-copy]].

---

## Editing Hierarchy

When editing, evaluate in this order. Do not skip to polish before truth is confirmed.

1. **Truth** — if the copy is misleading, nothing else matters.
2. **Clarity** — if the reader cannot understand it, it is not written yet.
3. **Concision** — if it can be shorter, shorten it.
4. **Voice** — if it is correct, clear, and concise but sounds like someone else, rewrite it.
5. **Polish** — rhythm, sound, and the pleasure of a well-built sentence. This is the last concern, not the first.

---

## Self-Editing Checklist

Before any Ponti Studios copy ships, run this checklist:

```
□ Is this true?
□ Can I prove it?
□ Can I say it with fewer words?
□ Would I say this to someone face to face?
□ Does it respect the reader's time?
□ Is the headline specific enough to survive alone?
□ Is every adjective doing work or decorating?
□ Does the tone match the context? (Web vs. product vs. social)
```

## The Cut List

These words and phrases are removed without hesitation. No exceptions in external copy.

### Fillers
- "Very," "really," "truly," "actually," "literally"

### Inflated verbs
- "In order to" → "to"
- "Utilize" → "use"
- "Leverage" as a verb meaning "use"

### Weasel nouns
- "Solution" as a synonym for "product"
- "Space" as in "the AI space"
- "Ecosystem" meaning "a few products we made"

### Self-importance
- "Empower" unless we are literally giving someone power
- "Innovative" — you do not get to call yourself innovative
- "Mission-driven" — your mission should be visible in your work, not your copy

## Quick Reference Cards

### Writing a Headline

```
□ Does it fit on one line?
□ Does it survive alone?
□ Is it a claim, not a question?
□ Is there a shorter way to say this?
```

### Writing a Description

```
□ Does the first sentence name what it is?
□ Does the second sentence name who it is for?
□ Is the mechanism explained without jargon?
□ Is the price or availability stated clearly?
```

### The Edit Pass

```
□ Delete every word that changes nothing when removed.
□ Replace every passive construction with an active one.
□ Read it aloud. If it trips, fix it.
□ Ask: would I send this to someone I respect?
```
