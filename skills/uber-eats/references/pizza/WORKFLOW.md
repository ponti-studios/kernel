# Pizza and Italian

Sourdough and wood-fired places are the best value on Uber Eats because the bundles discount hard — 20% off a two-pizza bundle with dips is usually the cheapest route to a full dinner. Anchovies are allowed here, so the whole menu is open apart from cured meat.

## Basket for two, £35–45

- A two-pizza bundle with dips and drinks, if the store runs one
- Otherwise: 2 pizzas, 1 bread or side, 1 dip each
- 1 dessert to share (tiramisu, cannolo)

## Order this

- **Pizzas:** margherita, marinara (vegan), ortolana, funghi, quattro formaggi, burrata, truffle and mushroom, potato and gorgonzola, Napoli (anchovies), tonno (tuna)
- **Sides:** garlic bread, garlic bread with mozzarella, focaccia, arancini, olives, rocket salad, burrata and tomatoes
- **Dips:** garlic, chilli, hot honey, truffle and grana, tomato pesto
- **Desserts:** tiramisu, cannolo, chocolate mousse, basque cheesecake

## Grep the menu for these words and skip them

`pepperoni`, `salami`, `spicy salami`, `prosciutto`, `parma`, `'nduja`, `nduja`, `sausage`, `bolognese`, `carbonara` (guanciale), `amatriciana` (guanciale), `mezzaluna` unless it names a vegetarian filling, `tre porcellini`.

## Traps

- **"Vegetariana" is the safe word** on Italian menus; ingredient lists are better still.
- **A "half pizza, half calzone" item usually hides meat** in one half — check both halves' fillings.
- **Bundle pickers sometimes exclude the premium pizzas.** Read the option list before promising truffle and burrata; the picker, not the menu page, defines what's allowed.
- **Dips are cheap and the bundle requires them.** Two dips at £2 each are worth taking inside the bundle even if you'd skip them à la carte.
- **Parmesan uses animal rennet.** The user is vegetarian but eats fish, so treat the restaurant's "(v)" marker as the authority and don't second-guess a marked dish.
- **Pizza travels well** — this is the safest cuisine for a 30-minute delivery. No need to hedge on the ETA.

## Rough prices

Pizzas £11–16 · sides £6–8 · dips £2 · desserts £6–7. Two pizzas, a side and two dips is £38–42 à la carte, which is exactly why a 20%-off bundle wins.
