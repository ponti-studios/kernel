# Middle Eastern

Mezze is the best-value way to feed two people: several small plates share better than two mains and cost less. Falafel and halloumi carry the vegetarian side; shawarma is the thing to avoid.

## Basket for two, £35–45

- 2 wraps or a falafel/halloumi plate each
- 2–3 mezze (hummus, baba ganoush, tabbouleh)
- 1 bread
- 1 sweet (baklava) if there's room

## Order this

- **Mains:** falafel wrap or plate, halloumi wrap, falafel and halloumi mixed grill, mujadara, spinach and feta fatayer
- **Mezze:** hummus, baba ganoush, moutabal, tabbouleh, fattoush, muhammara, vine leaves, labneh, pickles
- **Fish (allowed):** grilled sea bass, samke harra, prawn skewers
- **Bread:** flatbread, pitta, saj
- **Sweets:** baklava, kunafa, halva

## Grep the menu for these words and skip them

`shawarma`, `doner`, `kofta`, `kibbeh` (usually meat), `mixed grill` (unless all vegetarian), `lamb`, `chicken`, `beef`, `sujuk`, `maqluba` unless vegetarian.

## Traps

- **Hummus is often topped with lamb shawarma.** Ask for it plain, or check the description.
- **"Mixed grill" is a meat platter.** The vegetarian equivalent is usually called "falafel plate" or "vegetarian mezze".
- **Kibbeh and sambousek come in both meat and vegetarian forms.** Spinach or cheese sambousek is fine; the default is mince.
- **A falafel wrap alone is not dinner for two.** Order wraps plus mezze, or two plates.
- **Salads here are generous** — tabbouleh and fattoush are real dishes, not garnish, and are the cheapest way to fill the basket.

## Rough prices

Wraps and plates £9–12 · mezze £5–7 · bread £3 · baklava £4–5. Two mains, three mezze and bread lands £36–42 before fees.
