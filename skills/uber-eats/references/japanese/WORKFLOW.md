# Japanese and sushi

Sushi is the easiest cuisine on this list for this diet: fish is allowed, so the whole menu is open except the meat. Ramen is the trap, because the default broth is pork.

## Basket for two, £35–45

- 2 maki or inside-out rolls (or 1 roll plus sashimi)
- 6–8 nigiri pieces
- 1 hot side or small plate (edamame, gyoza, karaage alternative)
- 1 rice bowl or noodle dish if it's a ramen night instead
- miso soup if it's cheap

## Order this

- **Nigiri and sashimi:** salmon, tuna, yellowtail, prawn, mackerel, unagi (eel is fish)
- **Rolls:** avocado maki, cucumber maki, california roll (surimi is fish), salmon avocado, spicy tuna, inari
- **Small plates:** edamame, agedashi tofu, vegetable gyoza, seaweed salad, takoyaki (octopus), prawn tempura
- **Bowls:** vegetable or seafood ramen, vegetable katsu curry, yakisoba with vegetables, chirashi
- **Sides:** miso soup, pickles, rice

## Grep the menu for these words and skip them

`chicken`, `karaage` (fried chicken), `tonkotsu` (pork broth), `yakitori`, `katsu sando` (pork cutlet sandwich), `pork gyoza`, `beef`, `gyudon`, `unadon` is fine (eel), `chicken katsu`.

## Traps

- **Ramen broth is the hidden meat dish.** Tonkotsu is pork and shoyu is often chicken; choose a vegetable or seafood broth, or skip ramen and take a rice bowl.
- **"Katsu curry" is pork or chicken.** Ask for vegetable katsu — many places do a butternut or aubergine version.
- **"Chef's selection" and "deluxe platters" are built around the expensive fish**, which is fine here, but check whether a chicken or beef item is in the mix.
- **Order volume, not variety.** Sushi bills climb faster than any other cuisine: two rolls and eight nigiri plus a side is a full dinner for two, and adding a third roll is where £45 turns into £55.
- **Delivery sushi is warm.** Order sashimi and nigiri early in the evening rather than at closing time, and say so if it's late.

## Rough prices

Rolls £5–8 · nigiri £3–4 a piece · small plates £5–8 · ramen £12–15. Two rolls, eight nigiri pieces, edamame and miso lands £38–42 before fees.
