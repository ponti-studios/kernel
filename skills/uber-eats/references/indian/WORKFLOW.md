# Indian

Delivery Indian is a curry, a dal, bread and rice — and the vegetarian half of the menu is usually the better half. This is the easiest cuisine on the list to order well for two people.

## Basket for two, £35–45

- 2 curries: one dry or vegetable-led, one creamy
- 1 dal
- 2 breads plus 1 to share
- 1 rice to share
- 1 side (raita, chutney, papad)
- drinks only if a deal includes them

## Order this

- **Paneer:** butter masala, tikka masala, shahi paneer, palak paneer
- **Dal:** makhani, tadka, tarka — the cheapest good thing on any menu
- **Vegetable:** chana masala, chole, rajma, aloo gobi, baingan bharta, bhindi masala
- **Fish (allowed):** Goan fish curry, prawn korma, prawn moilee, prawn biryani, fish pakora
- **Bread:** garlic naan, peshwari naan, roti, laccha paratha
- **Rice:** pilau, jeera, plain basmati
- **Sides:** raita, mango chutney, lime pickle, papad

## Grep the menu for these words and skip them

`chicken`, `lamb`, `mutton`, `beef`, `keema`, `seekh`, `shish`, `mixed grill`, `house special`, `chef's special`, `tikka` unless it sits next to paneer.

## Traps

- **"Tikka masala" is chicken unless it says paneer.** The pink sauce is not a vegetarian signal; order "paneer tikka masala" by name.
- **"Saag" is ambiguous.** Saag paneer is safe; saag gosht and saag chicken are not.
- **Biryani is rarely vegetarian by default.** Choose veg biryani or prawn biryani, and check it isn't served with a separate meat curry.
- **Cheap takeaways share a base gravy between meat and veg dishes.** Fine for a vegetarian, not for someone avoiding meat stock — say so rather than pretend the kitchen is separate.
- **Naan is the best value filler** when the basket is £5 short of a promotion.

## Rough prices

Curries £9–13 · dal £6–8 · naan £3–4 · rice £3–4 · sides £3–4. Two curries, a dal, three breads and rice lands around £34–38 before fees, which leaves room for a deal-driven drink.
