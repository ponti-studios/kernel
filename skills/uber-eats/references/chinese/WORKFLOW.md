# Chinese

Chinese delivery splits into two very different menus: the Cantonese takeaway (stir-fries, rice, noodles) and the regional/dim-sum restaurant. The second is where the good vegetarian food is, and where the pork traps are.

## Basket for two, £35–45

- 2 mains (one tofu or aubergine, one noodle or rice-led)
- 1 dim sum or starter
- 1 green vegetable
- 1 rice
- drinks if a deal includes them

## Order this

- **Tofu:** salt and pepper tofu, mapo tofu (confirm no pork mince), home-style tofu, tofu claypot
- **Aubergine:** in garlic sauce, braised, "fish-fragrant" (the name is a cooking style, not an ingredient)
- **Greens:** pak choi, choi sum, garlic broccoli
- **Noodles and rice:** vegetable chow mein, Singapore noodles (veg), egg fried rice, plain rice
- **Dim sum:** vegetable gyoza, mushroom dumplings, vegetable spring rolls, cheung fun, har gow (prawn, allowed)
- **Fish (allowed):** prawn toast, salt and chilli prawns, steamed sea bass, prawn crackers

## Grep the menu for these words and skip them

`char siu`, `siu mai`, `xiao long bao`, `crispy duck`, `sweet and sour chicken`, `pork`, `beef ho fun`, `wonton` (usually pork), `hot and sour soup` (usually pork), `special` anything, `spare ribs`, `dan dan` unless it says vegetarian.

## Traps

- **"Mixed vegetables" can contain meat** in a cheap takeaway, and "special fried rice" almost always does.
- **Mapo tofu traditionally includes pork mince.** Ask for it without, or pick another tofu dish.
- **Sweet and sour and lemon chicken are the default "Chinese" dishes** — neither is vegetarian, and no, the sauce doesn't make up for it.
- **Dim sum is mostly pork and prawn.** The vegetarian set is small: gyoza, mushroom dumplings and spring rolls. Build the basket around mains, not dim sum.
- **Hot and sour soup and wonton soup are pork-based**; sweetcorn soup usually isn't.

## Rough prices

Mains £9–13 · dim sum £5–8 · greens £7–8 · rice £3–4. Two mains, a dim sum, greens and rice sits at £36–40 before fees.
