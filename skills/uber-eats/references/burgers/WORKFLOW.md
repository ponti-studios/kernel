# Burgers and fried chicken

This is the one cuisine where the store choice matters more than the dish choice. A fried-chicken specialist usually has no vegetarian main at all, and a burger chain that does a good plant patty beats a higher-rated chicken shop on this diet.

## Basket for two, £35–45

- 2 burgers (one plant-based or halloumi, one portobello or bean)
- 2 sides (fries plus one of onion rings, halloumi fries, slaw, mac and cheese)
- 2 drinks, or a shake to share
- 1 dip each if the deal requires it

## Order this

- **Burgers:** plant-based or beyond patty, halloumi burger, portobello mushroom burger, bean burger, buttermilk-fried cauliflower burger
- **Fish (allowed):** fish burger, fish and chips, prawn po' boy
- **Sides:** fries, halloumi fries, onion rings, mac and cheese, slaw, corn ribs, cauliflower wings
- **Extras:** milkshakes, pickles, cheese sauce, buffalo sauce

## Grep the menu for these words and skip them

`chicken`, `buttermilk chicken`, `wings` (unless cauliflower), `beef`, `smash patty`, `bacon`, `pulled pork`, `chorizo`, `doner`, `chicken salt` is only seasoning and is fine.

## Traps

- **Check the menu before choosing the store.** Fried-chicken chains often list a "veggie" option that is a wrap or a salad; if there is no proper vegetarian main, pick a different store rather than assembling a basket of sides.
- **"Plant-based" can mean cooked on the same grill as beef.** That matters to some people and not others — don't raise it unless the user has said.
- **Shakes and sides are where the bill grows.** Two burgers and two fries is £28; adding shakes and onion rings takes it past £40 quickly.
- **Meal deals usually bundle a meat burger.** Building two vegetarian items à la carte is often cheaper than a "2 for £X" that forces chicken.
- **Delivery fries go soft.** If the ETA is over 30 minutes, prefer a burger that survives the trip and skip the thin-cut fries.

## Rough prices

Burgers £8–13 · sides £4–7 · shakes £6–7. Two burgers and two sides lands £30–36 before fees, leaving room for one shake to share.
