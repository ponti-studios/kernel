# Mexican

Mexican delivery is beans, cheese, rice and tortillas — vegetarian-friendly by nature and priced by the item. The fish taco is the best non-vegetarian dish on most menus and is allowed here.

## Basket for two, £35–45

- 2 main items (burrito, bowl, or a pair of taco plates)
- 1 side of nachos or loaded fries to share
- 1 small (elote, jalapeño poppers, guacamole)
- drinks if a deal includes them

## Order this

- **Mains:** vegetable burrito, burrito bowl, quesadilla, enchiladas with beans, mushroom tacos, halloumi tacos, baja fish tacos
- **Fillings:** black beans, pinto beans, cheese, grilled peppers, corn, guacamole, sour cream, salsa, pickled onion
- **Sides:** nachos with cheese and beans, loaded fries, elote, jalapeño poppers, chips and guac
- **Desserts:** churros with chocolate (usually the best-value extra on the menu)

## Grep the menu for these words and skip them

`carnitas`, `barbacoa`, `al pastor`, `tinga`, `chorizo`, `beef`, `chicken`, `pork`, `birria`, `cochinita`.

## Traps

- **Refried beans are often made with lard.** Whole black beans are the safe choice; if the menu only offers refried, ask.
- **"Vegetarian" set meals sometimes default to chicken** when the kitchen runs the same line — read the item, not the section heading.
- **Nachos are a pricing trap.** A £9 plate of nachos and a £10 burrito is not dinner for two; add a second proper main.
- **Guacamole is charged per item** at most chains, so it is cheaper inside the burrito than as a side.
- **Salsa heat is not adjustable after the fact.** If the user says mild, choose the mild salsa in the options rather than hoping.

## Rough prices

Burritos and bowls £10–13 · taco plates £8–11 · nachos £7–9 · sides £4–6 · churros £5–6. Two mains, nachos and a small lands £36–42 before fees.
