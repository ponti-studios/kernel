pictures." The 6-head spec plus the painterly register's fashion-

Revised spec:

- **Petite: 5 heads tall, 153cm** (cast heights chart,
  `UNIVERSE.md` — LOCKED 17 Oct 2026, Chase's call; supersedes
  the 5.5-head / 160cm revision of earlier the same evening) —
  the shortest adult in the cast, roughly 90% of Lucy's height

  camera at her eye level or slightly above, never a low hero

  scale as the people around her — never foreground-enlarged.

  own proportions — they stand at EXACTLY the same height, eye
  line to eye line (cast heights chart, `UNIVERSE.md`; Wyatt

  deliberate change), an adult man level with her — never a
  height gap between them, never mascot scale.

## Failure modes (verify every render)

1. Face drift — fringe gone, hair centre-parted or wavy like
   Lucy's, eyes green, freckles appearing = fail. She is Lucy's
   negative: blue eyes, fringe, porcelain, no freckles, no coral.
   (Fringe gone is a fail in PUBLIC scenes; the at-home
   slicked-back look is the one canonical exception — Fixed
   Fact 4.)
2. Red drift — red dress/coat swapped, red turned flat cartoon
   red or pink, or red spreading to hair/lips in day scenes = fail.
3. Shoes — plain loafers, heels, boots, or trainers instead of the
   fur-trim horsebit loafers = fail.
4. Outfit drift — satin slip dress in a day scene, her Gucci
   pyjamas worn outdoors, or an outfit not from the wardrobe
   (default + rotation, this file) = fail.
5. Bearing — eager, smiling-for-camera, performing, or a
   blank, doll-like, younger anime face = fail. She is a
   temptress: heavy-lidded and knowing, above the spectacle,
   never part of it (blank-face ruling, Chase, 19 Oct 2026;
   her exact face is the photoreal face in `PHOTOREAL.md`,
   translated — see the references note).
6. Scale — taller than the men in a crowd scene, fashion-model
   leg elongation, low-angle hero framing, or foreground
   enlargement that makes her tower over the place = fail
   (Chase's correction, 17 Oct 2026: "too tall in all the
   pictures"). She is petite; the places are bigger than her.
7. Text on slides — universe rule: no words on slides; neon and
   signage must stay abstract glow, never readable lettering.
   (Exception, set 17 Oct 2026 for Carousel 9 v2: a real place's
   own signage/mural lettering, reproduced accurately, is the
   place's identity, not slide text.)
8. Pose lock — chin resting on her hand, any hand-to-face pose
   used as her default, the same pose repeated across a set,
   or a hand merged with her face = fail (Chase's correction,
   17 Oct 2026). Her boredom lives in her gaze and posture;
   poses vary frame to frame, and video keeps her hands off
   her face entirely.
