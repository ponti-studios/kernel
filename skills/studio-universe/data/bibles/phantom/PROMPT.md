# The Phantom — costume bible (Scooby set, "The Phantom of the Picture Palace")

> The phantom is a costume, not a citizen. Void wears it (over a stilted
> frame, ~230cm) in Frames 1 and 3; in Frame 4 it lies collapsed and its
> mask is held high by Wyatt. This file is the single home of the costume
> prose. The core block goes into every frame the costume appears in; at
> most one named tail is appended per frame, picked by the frame spec.

## PHANTOM BLOCK (stitch verbatim into every prompt the costume appears in)

"The phantom is a towering figure about 230cm tall: a floor-length hooded robe of layered dusty grey-white sheet gauze that pools and trails at the floor; a smooth blank oval face of matte light grey, featureless except two deep hollow black oval eyes and a small dark open oval mouth — plainly a theatrical costume mask; very long draped sleeves that completely hide the hands; a faint haze of pale grey dust around its hem. It is a person in a Halloween costume: a solid figure standing on its own feet, the robe dense opaque cloth with its hem resting on the floor, dressed for a harmless theatrical fright."
