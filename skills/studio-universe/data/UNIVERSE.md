## The colour engine (established 16 Oct 2026 — building, Chase's calls)

The shared mechanic that defines Lucy, Void, and Monotone's place
between them. This file owns the engine's rules; each character's
bible will own how the engine is rendered on that character.

Locked so far:

1. **Monotone is immune.** His red never drains — including inside
   Void's colour-drain halo, where everything around him can be
   greying. He is not Lucy's creation and she does not maintain him;
   his red is his by nature (it predates her — he has had the same red
   head since Chase first drew him in high school). Lucy is the one
   who works to give the rest of the world what he has for free.
2. **The drain persists.** Colour Void drains does not return on its
   own. A drained place stays grey until Lucy designs it back — her
   labour is the only way back. So every location in this world sits
   in one of these states: untouched colour, draining (Void present),
   grey awaiting Lucy, or restored by her hand.
3. **Her work can be re-drained.** Void can drain what Lucy has
   restored, so nothing she ships is permanent — it is only
   currently live, and she maintains it forever. The engine is a cycle,
   not a campaign: no final victory, no territory held for good. The
   rollback always comes; she ships anyway.
4. **Void is a schemer, not a weather event (Chase, 20 Oct 2026).**
   When Void is the villain of a story, he arrives with a NEW
   scheme fitted to that place and that story — Dick Dastardly
   rules: a menace with a plan, foiled by the gang, back next time
   with a different one. Colour-draining is his signature power
   and his favourite tool, and it stays available to any scheme
   (and to his background dread appearances) — but "Void drains a
   place and Lucy restores it" is ONE plot, not his plot every
   time. Vary his objective, his method and his target from story
   to story; a Void appearance that is just a drain with new
   scenery fails this rule.

**Reframe note (17 Oct 2026, Chase — full reframe):** Lucy is a
software designer, so restoration is design work. She treats the
city as an interface: a drained place is a greyed-out, broken
screen, and her fix is a design she ships on the spot — tablet
and stylus, palettes and tokens, colour blooming back where she
works (the production rule below is unchanged: the bloom is
composited radially around her). Coral #EE7B57 is her signature
design token. Sets posted before the reframe (Carousels 4, 5, 7,
8) show her painting; that depiction stands historically, the
way earlier designs do.
**Street amendment (19 Oct 2026, Chase):** the tablet is studio
kit. When Lucy is in the streets she is a street artist — she
does not bring her iPad outside, and street restoration is
hands-on work: her mark, laid directly on the surface, and the
colour comes back where her line lands. The design framing
(interface, tokens, the coral signature) is how she thinks;
the street execution is paint and marker, not a screen.

  liked in the right rooms.
- **Void — Finance Director.** Scarcity with a spreadsheet.
  Budgets enter in full colour and leave grey, drained to what
  they actually need. Nobody enjoys his meetings; the studio
  never overspends.
- **Akane — Director of Taste.** She makes nothing and
  approves everything. Work is pitched to her and her boredom
  is the quality bar: if she looks up, it ships; if she does
  not, it dies quietly. Wyatt brings her roses and drafts, in

  wellbeing, walks, and final say on snacks.

The operating pattern: Wyatt builds, Monotone defines, Lucy
maintains, Benny connects, Void constrains, Akane judges,
Cyndi keeps everyone human.

## How to generate (reliability rule — learned from the 15 Oct universe test)

Solo generations from each character's own bible passed clean. The
crossover, built from a COMPRESSED summary of the chibi spec, failed on
exactly the failure mode his bible predicts (perforated mask). Therefore:
**prompts are assembled from the verbatim blocks, never from summaries** —
Monotone's MASTER BLOCK v2 + proportions block word-for-word, and the
chibi's mask negative block word-for-word, in solo and crossover prompts
alike. This file routes; the bibles speak verbatim.
Two refinements from the 5-scenario trio tests (15 Oct): (1) the chibi's
mask perforations creep in when he is SMALL in frame — compose group shots
with him large, and lead his description with "completely smooth,
unperforated mask surface" before the verbatim block (the phrasing
"a completely smooth unperforated black shell like a fencing mask"
worked on the 15 Oct night-flash re-roll after the standard lead-in
failed twice); always state "4:5" explicitly in trio prompts — without
it the model returns 9:16; (2) Monotone's build
is an ordinary adult build — say so, or fitness scenes will bulk him.

## Crossover rules

1. Each character keeps his own spec in a shared frame — the mask's voids
   stay voids, Monotone's piecut eyes stay piecut. Never blend features.
2. Each keeps his own proportions — the chibi is chibi, Monotone is five
   heads tall. Do not normalise them to each other; the height difference
   is part of the universe's logic.
3. Cyndi appears once, at the scale of whoever she is standing with
   (beside Monotone: the prompt system's PROPORTIONS section; beside the chibi: chibi bible).
4. Neither character speaks on slides. Story is carried by placement,
   pose and scene.
5. Benny, Lucy and Void's bibles locked 16 Oct 2026, so crossovers
   are open to them; rules 1–2 apply to them the same way — own
   spec, own proportions, never blended.
