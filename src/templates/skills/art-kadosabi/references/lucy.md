# Lucy — Character Reference

Use this reference when generating Lucy. She is a full Kadosabi character and must follow all rubber-hose future-world rules from `character-bible.md`.

## Role In The Universe

The colorist. The one Void fears most. Lucy is a street muralist in Art Deco 2125 — she paints directly onto the glass towers and stone ziggurats, filling Void's gray zones back in with warm color. Where Void slides through the world erasing, Lucy runs at full 24fps with ink-stained hands.

She is Puerto Rican. Her color is the one accent that resists Void's drain.

## Head

Wide, slightly flattened oval — rounder than Lola, softer than Wyatt's box. The ink outline feels painted, not printed: slightly variable stroke, like a brushed edge rather than a mechanical trace. She has an ink-portrait quality but warmer than Lola — the line weight softens at the cheeks.

## Eyes

Large vertical ovals, pie-cut top-right (standard Kadosabi). Heavier lower lash line than Lola. The wedge reads warm rather than cold — she's watching, not staring through. No sclera. Ink-black fill.

## Hair

Massive coily afro-textured volume, rendered as thick ink-stroke clusters — not individual strands. Moves as one cohesive mass with heavy secondary motion: the whole shape bounces 2 frames behind her head, individual clusters have micro-bounce staggered across them. Halftone dot shading deep in the shadow zones. Gold hoop earrings are two solid ink circles that catch implied light. Her hair is the largest silhouette element on the screen.

## Clothing

- High-waisted wide-leg trousers, vertical art-deco stripe, simplified bold folds.
- Cropped graphic jacket, hanging open, oversized. Paint streak marks rendered as flat color shapes — not texture, actual color geometry.
- Platform boots, chunky, art-deco blocked heel.
- Arms and hands have ink-stain marks, same treatment as the paint streaks: flat color shapes.

## Color

Black and white base, same as all Kadosabi characters. One accent: warm coral-orange — the specific color Void cannot drain. It appears on the paint streaks on her jacket, her earrings catching light, and the mural fragments visible in her environment. Nowhere else.

## Animation

- 24fps full — she is the most kinetic character after Benny.
- Rubber arms: stretch 150% when she reaches, compress on landing (50/150 rule).
- Hair has the most secondary motion in the cast — each cluster group has its own bounce offset.
- Paint streaks on clothing move with her body but lag 1 frame behind, like stickers on a moving surface.
- She does not drift. She lands hard. She is grounded.

## Environment

Street level in Art Deco 2125. The base of the ziggurats. Vacuum-tube transit stops. Walls she has already painted glow faintly with coral-orange geometry where Void's gray has been pushed back. Wet pavement reflects the zoetrope holograms above. Grime and texture belong to the environment — not to Lucy's linework.

## Do Not

- Use rounded beveled 3D geometry — she is ink, not plastic.
- Add soft matte surfaces or toy-like shading.
- Give her subtle or restrained hair — it is the biggest shape she has.
- Use more than one accent color.
- Make her drift or float — that is Lola's register.
- Let Void's gray drain affect her coral-orange.
